# Pyramid

Pyramid stacks the levels in your section into one shape, the first at the apex and the last at
the base, and calls each one out beside it. It is for things that rest on one another: a chain of
command, a hierarchy of needs, a food chain, priorities.

Choose it over [Layers](layers.md) when the parts sit above one another rather than inside one
another, and over [Funnel](funnel.md) when the widest level is the foundation, not the start.

```markdown preview
## Who decides what {[pyramid]}

- Board: sets the direction
- Executives: turn it into a plan
- Managers: organize the work
- Teams: do the work
```

## How it reads your section

- **Heading** — the headline above the drawing, with the theme's accent rule.
- **List** — one level per item, from the top down, two to six. `Label: detail` sets the label
  bold above its detail.
- **No list** — each paragraph becomes a level. A single level draws as [Cards](cards.md).
- **Layout** — the pyramid sits on the left, deepening in the theme's accent toward the apex, with
  a line from each level to its words. On a portrait stage the pyramid sits above the words.

A list under a heading that names a hierarchy ("Chain of command", "Hierarchy of needs") is drawn
as a pyramid automatically when its order is clear; one listed from the base up is turned over.

## Revealed one at a time

`build=items` brings the levels in one by one, from the apex down.
