# Block gallery

A **block** is one section of your document: a heading and everything under it, up to the next
heading at the same level or higher. Each block renders through a **template**, and you choose the
template with a tag at the end of the heading:

```markdown
## Our biggest year yet {[statHighlight]}

**3×** growth in active readers since January.
```

Leave the tag off and Squiggly Square picks a sensible template for you — a table becomes a data
table, a lone image becomes an image block, a list becomes a list (see
[automatic templates](../guide/markup.md#automatic-templates)). Add a tag when you want a
particular look.

Every block below links to a page with a live example, the parameters its tag accepts, and tips
for using it well. Previews use the document's own theme; switch themes from any preview to see
how a block adapts.

> [!TIP]
> Templates differ in how much of the section they show. Content-first blocks such as
> [Content](content.md) and [List](list.md) show everything you write; display blocks such as
> [Stat Highlight](statHighlight.md) or [Quote](quote.md) pick out one striking thing. The rest
> stays in your document — it appears in the Document view and in Word and PDF exports. Each page
> says which kind it is under **Body**.
