# Hub

Hub draws the ideas in your section around a single centre, each joined to it by a spoke. It is
for the several causes of one event, the factors behind one result, the parts of one system.

Choose it over [Cards](cards.md) when the ideas all bear on one thing, and over
[Cycle](cycle.md) when they do not follow one another.

```markdown preview
## What drives the tides {[hub center="The tides"]}

- The Moon: its pull raises the water facing it
- The Sun: adds to the Moon's pull, or works against it
- Earth's spin: carries each coast through two bulges a day
- The coastline: bays and channels pile the water higher
```

## How it reads your section

- **Heading** — the headline above the drawing, with the theme's accent rule.
- **`center`** — the one to three words written inside the hub. Without it the hub is a plain disc
  and the heading names what the ideas share.
- **List** — one idea per item, two to six. `Label: detail` sets the label bold above its detail.
- **No list** — each paragraph becomes an idea. A single idea draws as [Cards](cards.md).
- **Layout** — the ideas read down the left of the hub, then down the right. On a portrait stage
  the hub sits at the left and the ideas fan out beside it.

A list of labelled items under a heading that names what they share ("Causes of…", "Factors
behind…", "Pillars of…") is drawn as a hub automatically.

## Revealed one at a time

`build=items` draws each spoke out from the hub and brings in its idea, one by one. The hub is
there from the start.
